How to install

  1. Tap Download PiFoto and open the file when it's done.
  2. If Android says it can't install apps from your browser, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  3. Tap Install (or Update), then open PiFoto and log in with your name and password. No account yet? Open the invite link you were sent first.

After that the app keeps itself up to date. The first update asks once to allow installs from PiFoto.
